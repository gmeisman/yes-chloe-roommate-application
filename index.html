Question 2 of 2

Why would you be a good roommate?

"I believe that I would be a fantastic roommate because I already have a deep and vested interest in the current tenant. I want to see you succeed and happy." Here's what that actually looks like, broken down.

Open, honest, and frequent

My goal is to just be open and honest and talk to you as often as possible. If I do that, I don't foresee any problems even if there's a disagreement, because I know communication matters to you, and I know you're happier faster when I talk to you more. So I'll aim to talk frequently, and tell you as soon as I find anything in the house that needs attention.

Aware, tidy, and accountable

I would be aware of where I am and what I'm leaving behind to make sure I don't leave messes. I'll set alerts and keep a chart on the fridge as a reminder of what still needs to be cleaned and duties that need to be done — chores completed in a reasonable time, to your standards or greater.

Three meals a week, guaranteed

I'm willing to make three meals a week. If I can't make one for whatever reason, I'll either get you food, give you the necessary funds if you're home and I'm not, or have a premade meal ready with prior communication saying so. No silent no-shows at dinner.

Dancer, masseur, and negotiable extras

I'm also a great dancer and masseur and am happy to provide these services to you from time to time. Other services aren't off the table either, we can discuss those when the time comes.

Dear Ms Brew,

I understand you have requested a formal letter of recommendation for Garren Meisman, despite the fact that you are already engaged to him and have had years to conduct your own highly invasive background investigation.

Nevertheless, I am happy to provide my strongest possible recommendation.

After extensive observation, family review, character assessment, and repeated real-world testing, I have determined that Garren is exceptionally qualified for the highly prestigious position of Person Allowed to Live in the Room.

Garren is responsible, respectful, hardworking, dependable, intelligent, and trustworthy. He pays his bills, keeps his commitments, handles his responsibilities, and generally functions like a competent adult without requiring a laminated instruction card.

More importantly, I would personally trust Garren with:

  • My house
  • My car
  • My money
  • My dog
  • My credit card
  • My passwords, if absolutely necessary
  • An emergency situation
  • Important family decisions
  • The last good piece of steak in the refrigerator
  • And, apparently, my daughter

That last one is the most important.

If I am willing to trust the man with my daughter for the rest of his natural life, I feel reasonably confident he can be trusted with one bedroom and access to a refrigerator.

In fact, from a risk-management standpoint, the bedroom is substantially less valuable and considerably easier to replace.

Garren has also demonstrated extraordinary patience, emotional stability, conflict-management skills, and long-term endurance through his relationship with you.

This should not be overlooked.

Any man who can date you, survive the evaluation period, propose voluntarily, and then apparently submit supporting documentation just to live in the same house has already exceeded any reasonable standard of character.

For purposes of addressing the allegations you will inevitably submit after reading this letter:

If Garren leaves a cup on the counter, this does not constitute gross negligence.

If he forgets something you mentioned once three weeks ago while he was watching television, this does not establish a pattern of misconduct.

If he loads the dishwasher incorrectly according to standards that exist exclusively inside your head, corrective action should begin with verbal counseling.

And if your response to any of this is, "Dad, you don't know what he's really like," I would respectfully remind you that I raised you.

I know exactly how this works.

My formal assessment is therefore as follows:

I don't want to just pass your screening. I want to be someone you're glad said yes.

So live with me. I'll bring the chore chart, the three weekly meals, the dance moves, and the headphones for after nine.
